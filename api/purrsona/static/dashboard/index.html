Writing rules

How you write, by who it's for. The narrowest rule of each kind wins: a person beats their circle, a circle beats a topic, a topic beats a platform, and those beat your everywhere habits. Forget any rule with ✕: it's retired (kept as history) and the key remembers that you said so.

What the key learned

Every memory on your key, newest first. Each one is replayed into the graph when you plug in, so unplugging never loses it.

Handoffs

Three agents, one shared memory. They never call each other: the Drafter writes from the graph's rules, the Critic checks the draft against them and hands it back once if it broke one, and the Learner turns what you sent into new rules. Every step is a node in the graph.

Key & privacy

Your voice lives on your key. Plug it in and the laptop builds your graph; unplug it and the graph is deleted.

Your key

What leaves your laptop

For each draft, Claude gets one small brief: the post you're replying to (or your own note) and the few rules the graph picked. Never the whole graph, and never a private conversation.

Nothing is read in the background: the cat reads a page only when you press ⌥M, click it, or send. It never posts for you.
